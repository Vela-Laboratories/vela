---
title: "Portfolio estimates"
description: "Read fill-based PnL, open lots, and export your fill history."
---

`GET /portfolio/{address}?method=fifo` requires an account-bound bearer
session. Use `method=hifo` to consume the highest-cost lot first instead.
The response includes realized and unrealized PnL, a per-market breakdown,
and remaining open lots. All USDC values are decimal strings, not micro-USDC
integers. Lot quantities are decimal base-asset units and `acquired_at` is a
Unix timestamp in seconds.

These figures are testnet estimates reconstructed from recorded fills. Open
positions use current order-book marks. A per-market `unrealized_usdc` value
is `null` when no mark is available; aggregate unrealized and combined PnL
exclude that market. The page at `/portfolio` flags this condition.
Each market also reports `mark_source` (`midpoint`, `best_bid`, or `best_ask`)
and `mark_observed_at_ms`. The timestamp is when the book was read, not proof
that the resting quote was recently placed.
If a sell has no matching retained acquisition fill, its market reports
`pnl_complete: false` and `unmatched_sell_quantity_base`. Fills also retain
their signed maker and taker fees; a legacy fill without fee evidence reports
`fee_complete: false` and makes PnL incomplete. Buy fees enter lot cost basis,
and sell fees reduce realized proceeds. The page hides PnL totals while any
market is incomplete. The API's numeric fields remain provisional in that case.

`GET /portfolio/{address}/csv` returns a CSV of the account's fills. Its
columns are `timestamp_ms`, `market`, `side`, `role`, `price_usdc`,
`quantity_base`, `notional_usdc`, `fee_usdc`, `counterparty`, and `fill_id`.
`fee_usdc` is blank for older fills without retained fee evidence; a negative
value is a rebate. It is a fill-history export,
not a tax-lot or tax-filing export. It requires the same bearer session.
